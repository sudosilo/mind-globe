Mind Globe

Mind Globe is for people who want to see their ideas as a place.

A 3D globe shows terrain, and an atlas view lists your notes. A new note takes a region, the text, and the size of the idea, and raising land adds an island to the globe. Notes save to the backend and pins appear on the globe. Zoom buttons work, and delete needs a second tap to confirm.

Live at ming-globe.vercel.app
